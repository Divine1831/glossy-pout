Meet the founder

A little idea. A whole lot of heart.

Christabel Okoh started Glossy Pout in 2025 in Benin City, Edo State, with a simple belief: a little everyday beauty should feel joyful, personal and accessible. What began as an idea grew into a community, and by late 2025 Glossy Pout had welcomed its first 100 customers. Every product is part of the same promise: thoughtful little essentials that let you make your look your own.

Our mission

Everyday glow, made accessible.

To make lip care and feel-good beauty essentials easier to enjoy, every day.

Our vision

A favourite across West Africa.

To grow into a trusted lip-care favourite known for thoughtful products, joyful self-expression and a signature shine.
