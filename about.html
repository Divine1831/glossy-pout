MEET THE FOUNDER

A little idea.
A whole lot of heart.

Christabel Okoh started Glossy Pout in 2025 in Benin City, Edo State, with a simple belief: a little everyday beauty should feel joyful, personal and accessible. What began as an idea grew into a community, and by late 2025 Glossy Pout had welcomed its first 100 customers.

Every product is part of the same promise — thoughtful little essentials that let you make your look your own.
