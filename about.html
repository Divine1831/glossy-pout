MEET THE FOUNDER

A little idea with a lot of heart.

Christabel Okoh started Glossy Pout in 2025 with one goal: to create beautiful lip products that help people feel confident, radiant and pampered. From its beginnings in Benin City, Glossy Pout continues to grow one shade and one customer at a time.
