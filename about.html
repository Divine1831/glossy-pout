MEET THE FOUNDER

A little idea.
A whole lot of heart.

Christabel Okoh founded Glossy Pout in Benin City, Edo State, with a simple belief: everyday beauty should feel joyful, personal and accessible. What began as an idea grew into a community of lip lovers, with the first 100 customers joining the story in late 2025.

From lip gloss and lip care to the little tools that complete a look, every product is chosen to help you express your own signature.
